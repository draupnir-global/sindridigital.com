Crypto-asset recovery services.

Sindri Digital GmbH provides services in relation to crypto-asset recovery, delivered through certified Swiss forensics partners. Services are provided directly to natural and legal persons.

LUGANO, SWITZERLAND
WHAT WE DO

The model.

Before entering into a business relationship, Sindri Digital requests blockchain due-diligence reports from a specialised Third-Party Service Provider to verify the client’s ownership of the relevant crypto assets. Our forensics partner is Recoveris, a Swiss blockchain-forensics firm. The Third-Party Service Provider investigates each matter and prepares a report on a white-label basis. Sindri Digital provides the resulting report to the client in its own name and under its own branding, under a Service Agreement. The client may pay in fiat or in crypto assets.

BOUNDARIES

What our services do not include.

Sindri Digital’s services do not include the actual recovery of crypto assets. Sindri Digital does not initiate legal proceedings, does not undertake or arrange technical measures such as hacking, does not alert third-party service providers such as crypto exchanges, and does not engage private investigators or law-enforcement agencies.

Sindri Digital does not carry out any financial-intermediary activity subject to a regulatory licence. It does not accept, safeguard or hold client crypto assets; does not assist clients in investing or managing crypto assets; and does not assist clients in transferring crypto assets. Sindri Digital may accept crypto assets from clients as consideration for its services.

OUR GROUP

Sindri Digital within Draupnir Global

Sindri Digital is the digital asset arm of the Draupnir Global group, headquartered in Lugano. We operate our own Bitcoin and digital asset holdings alongside the client-facing forensics practice — separate banking, separate compliance infrastructure, shared precision.

Own holdings
